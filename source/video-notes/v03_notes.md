## Topic
EN: Before closing, make every tube a true full stack: run a straight line across the tops, swap a doubtful stack with one you already proved, and take chips only from the partials. Partials live on the right (white is the exception). Fill the rack from bottom to top, inside out, so the floor sees complete straight rows. Recap of rundown: pink/green/purple by 4s, red/black by 5s. Then: "Now we're going to close the game."
ZH: 收台前把筹码槽摆整齐：用一条直线看齐，用已经确认的满摞去换、核对还没确认的摞，缺的只从零头里补。零头放右侧（白筹除外）。从下到上、从里到外填满，方便楼面一眼看清。拆法复习：粉/绿/紫按 4，红/黑按 5。然后开始收台。
