## Topic
EN: Opening a game (the counterpart to the close chain). Floor unlocks the lid, brings out cards, shoe, signs and tape; you are told to take the lid off and put it away. Only full stacks come out first. Always keep the biggest cheques closest to you (if someone snatches, they should have to reach past the high value). Break down the nearest stack by 4s to prove it; once one stack is proved, anything even and touching it is also proved. Swap an unproved stack with a proved one to finish the rack without breaking every stack. Then the floor asks you to read each color's total without touching the cheques. Full-stack / mini-stack values used in class: purple mini $2,000; black full $2,000 / mini $500; green full $500 / mini $100; red full $100 / mini $25; pink full $50 / mini $10; white full $20 / mini $5. Class totals: purple $22,000, black ~$11,300, green $3,775, red $1,150, pink $17.50, white $24, quarters $1.50. Floor signs with badge number; dealer double-checks game / pit / table (the open date may not be the same day you later close — a game can sit unopened for a week).
ZH: 开台盘点（关台链的对应课）。楼面开盖、拿出牌、牌靴、指示牌和胶带，让你把盖子收好。先只拿出满垛。最大面额要始终靠近自己（防顺手牵羊）。靠自己最近的那垛按 4 拆开证明；一旦一垛被证明，靠齐并贴着它的垛也算已证明。把未证明的垛与已证明的垛对调，不必每垛都拆。然后楼面问你各颜色总额，不许再碰筹码。课堂满垛/小垛：紫小垛 $2,000；黑满 $2,000 / 小 $500；绿满 $500 / 小 $100；红满 $100 / 小 $25；粉满 $50 / 小 $10；白满 $20 / 小 $5。课堂总额：紫 $22,000、黑约 $11,300、绿 $3,775、红 $1,150、粉 $17.50、白 $24、quarter $1.50。楼面签字并写工号；经销商再核对游戏、坑、台号（开台日期不一定是关台那天，台子可能一周后才开）。
