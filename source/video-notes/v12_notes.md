## Topic
EN: Intro to splits. A split turns one hand into two completely separate hands. The new bet must equal the original (not less, not more). The two cards must have the same point value (2-2 yes, 7-2 no; any two ten-value cards yes). Get the two-finger V signal first because the player still has a choice (split or double); if they don't signal, ask "Sir, what would you like to do?" Put the new bet on the right and prove it for the camera, even if it is one cheque. The original card stays with the original bet; separate the hands. A player may split to a total of four hands (three splits). Do not overlap cards. On the first two cards you must see all the pips; once a seat has many split hands you have a little leeway as long as the cards stay readable and not overlapping. After taking a losing bet (money first, top of the rack), lock up the hand and move on.
ZH: 分牌入门：一手分成完全独立的两手。补注必须与原注相等（不能少也不能多）。两张牌必须点数相同（2+2 可以，7+2 不行；两张 10 点牌不论花色点数都可以分）。先看两根手指的 V 手势（客人还有得选：分或加倍）；没有手势就问 Sir, what would you like to do? 新注放右侧并向镜头证明，哪怕只有一张筹码。原牌跟着原注走，两手要分开。最多一共 4 手（分三次）。牌不能重叠。前两张必须露出所有点数（pips）；后来分很多手、台面挤的时候，只要能看清、不重叠，可以稍有通融。收输注时先拿钱放到筹码槽顶部，再锁牌往下走。
