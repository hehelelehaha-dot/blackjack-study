## Topic
EN: Blackjack 3:2 payouts: memorize pays for easy amounts ($40 pays $60, $50 pays $75, $15 pays $22.50), and if you don't know $55, break it into $50+$5. Cut out what it *pays*, not what the player "gets" (don't add the original bet into the cut). Then thumb-roll, biggest cheques that way.
