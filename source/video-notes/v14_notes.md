## Topic
EN: Aces may be split only once and each hand gets one card, placed sideways so the camera and the floor can see there is no further hit. Don't wait for a hand signal (the player has no choice). A split ace plus a ten is 21, not a blackjack — do not pay 3:2. Experienced players may ask for the card face down; only do that if they ask. After the dealer breaks, flip one hand at a time so the player sees it, then pay / take / push one bet at a time, transparently, and prove the bet. No overlapping cards.
ZH: A 只能分一次，每手只给 1 张，而且要横着放，让镜头和楼面看出不能再要牌。不用等手势（客人没有选择）。分 A 后再来一张 10 是 21，不是 Blackjack，不能按 3:2 赔。有经验的客人可能要求牌面朝下，只有对方提出才这么做。荷官爆牌后一次翻一手给客人看，再按手赔/收/和，动作要让所有人看见，并证明注码。牌不能重叠。
