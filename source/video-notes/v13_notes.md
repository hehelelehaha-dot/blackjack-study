## Topic
EN: Split procedure and table manners: keep hands readable (no overlapping cards), a player may split to a total of four hands, only the first two equal cards, equal money, new bet on the right, prove every bet. After "no more bets" and the first card, bets are locked and players may not touch cards or bets. Correct guests politely. The split signal is a V (two fingers), not one finger.
ZH: 分牌：牌面不能重叠、最多一共 4 手、只能分最开始两张点数相同的牌、补同等注、新注放右侧并向镜头证明。喊过 no more bets 且第一张牌出来后，客人不能再碰注或碰牌。分牌手势是 V（两根手指）。语气要客气。
