## Topic
EN: Turning Stone allows a double down on any first two cards (except 21), for one card only, up to the original bet (for less yes, for more no). The new bet goes on the right and must be proved (size in; just setting it down is not a proof). A hand signal is required when the player still has a choice (a pair could be split or doubled). If they simply put money down on a non-pair, that is the signal. Move money that was stacked on top of the original bet. An irregular double (hard 12) is a "red flag": announce it the same way you announce "Shuffle!" or "last hand" — not asking permission, notifying the team.
ZH: Turning Stone 允许任意前两张加倍（21 点除外），只给 1 张牌；金额可以少于原注，不能多于原注。新注放右侧并 size in 证明。对子既可分也可加倍时必须看手势/问清楚；若不是对子、客人把钱放下，这本身就是信号。钱如果叠在原注上要挪到右侧。不常见的加倍（如 hard 12）是 red flag，要像喊 Shuffle / last hand 一样大声告知楼面，不是在请示。
