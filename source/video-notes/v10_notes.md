## Topic
EN: Closing paperwork and security: the supervisor adds the closer in their head, signs name and badge number, then the dealer checks the math (a suit can still be wrong), signs underneath with their dealer badge number, seats the slip over the white and red, puts the lid on, and waits for the pit boss. No one else's hands go in the dealer's rack. Pit may point with a pen. Hand the closer by setting it on the table, not into his hands. After pit initials, lid on, double-check, clap and show your hands, then lock off the game.
ZH: 收台文书：主管心算签字（姓名+工牌号），荷官也要自己验算再签在下面；单据压在白筹和红筹上，盖盖，等坑主。任何人不能把手伸进你负责的筹码槽（主管最多用笔指）。把 closer 放桌上递，不要塞进对方手里。坑主草签后盖盖、再核对，拍掌亮手，然后锁台下班。
