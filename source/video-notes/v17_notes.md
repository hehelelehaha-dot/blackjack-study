## Topic
EN: If you pay in the same color, just size in (no need to break the bet down). If you pay in a different color, break the bet down and use "total look back" so the camera can tell the original bet from the payout (leave a space between them).
ZH: 同色赔付只需 size in；如果改用另一种颜色赔，必须先把原注拆开，再用 total look back（原注和赔付中间留空）让镜头分清哪一摞是注、哪一摞是赔。
